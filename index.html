Chapter 01 · Automations & Systems

Where I map a messy process and build the system that runs it.

The unglamorous stuff — the spreadsheet nobody trusts, the update that's always a day late. I like turning "someone does this by hand every week" into "this just happens now."

Case 01 / 04

Shopify → Monday.com Order Sync

Make.comWebhooksShopify Flow
Shopify
→
Make.com
→
Monday.com
Problem

Order data was scattered across Shopify, vendor confirmation emails, and a manually updated board — updates lagged and errors crept in.

Approach

Built a Make.com pipeline triggered by vendor emails and Shopify order events, parsing data and syncing status to Monday.com in real time.

Outcome

Eliminated manual entry, removed sync lag, and gave the team one reliable source of truth for order status.

Shopify Theme Customization

Problem

A purchased theme looked fine out of the box, but didn't match the brand direction or how the team actually needed to work.

Approach

Went into the theme code directly — Liquid, CSS, and JS — to adjust layout, styling, and functionality against specific requests, not just settings-panel tweaks.

Outcome

A site that looks custom without a custom price tag, and one I can keep adjusting myself instead of waiting on a developer.
